The Cloud Who Tucked the Sky In

Puff was a small cloud, soft and round as a pillow.

All afternoon, Puff had drifted over the meadow. The grass was sleepy and the wind was slow.

"Drift, little cloud, drift softly on," Puff whispered to itself.

The sun felt warm on Puff's puffy top. Whoosh, went the wind. Whoosh, went the wind again.

Below, a little green pond sparkled. A young frog sat on a lily pad, pulling more pads close.

"What are you making?" asked Puff.

"A nest," said the frog. "But it is hot, and I am tired."

Puff floated over the pond. Puff made a patch of cool shade. Then Puff sent a gentle breeze across the water. Ffft, ffft, went the breeze.

"Ahh," sighed the frog. "That is much better."

"Drift, little cloud, drift softly on," Puff sang, and off Puff went.

Up the hill, a sheep was tucking wool around its round body. It lay under a tall tree and yawned a big, woolly yawn.

"Phew," said the sheep. "This sun is making my wool itchy."

Puff drifted softly above the tree. Puff made a shady spot, soft and gray, like a blanket laid just for the sheep.

The sheep sighed. "Oh, that feels lovely. Thank you, cloud."

"Drift, little cloud, drift softly on," Puff hummed, very low.

The sky turned peach and gold. Puff floated over a row of rooftops. In one roof, a family of birds was settling into their nest.

"Hush, hush, little ones," said the mother bird.

Puff hummed a low, soft wind song. Hmmmm, went the wind. Hmmmm, went the wind.

One bird tucked its head under its wing. Then another. Then the last little bird, yawn, and sleepy eyes.

"Drift, little cloud, drift softly on," Puff whispered.

Puff drifted up, up, into the evening sky, where the peach and gold had faded to deep, sleepy blue. There was the moon, peeking out. A few early stars blinked hello.

Puff spread out, thin and soft, like a quilt over the whole sky.

The world grew quiet.

"Drift, little cloud, drift softly on," Puff said, very, very softly.

The pond grew still. The frog closed its eyes.

The sheep breathed slow and deep.

The birds' nest rustled, rustle, shh.

The moon glowed warm and silver.

Puff floated low, and slow, and soft.

Drift, little cloud.

Drift softly on.
