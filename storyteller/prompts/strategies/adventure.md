Strategy for adventure stories:
- Shape the middle as a small quest with a clear goal the child can hold in mind.
- Use the rule of three: the hero meets three obstacles or tries three times, and each try teaches something that helps with the next.
- Excitement comes from wonder and discovery (a glowing cave, a talking river), never from danger or fear.
- Let a helper or companion appear, but the hero makes the decisive choice.
- After the goal is reached, the journey home is short and cozy, leading into the wind-down.
