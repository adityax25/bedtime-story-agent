Strategy for funny and silly stories:
- Start from one absurd premise and treat it completely seriously; the contrast is the joke.
- Escalate: each scene gets a little sillier than the last, ideally in three steps.
- Give a character a catchphrase or running gag, and let the refrain be the funniest line.
- Use playful sounds, made-up words, and situations a child can picture vividly.
- Humor is never mean; nobody is laughed at for being different. After the biggest laugh, the silliness winds down into giggly, yawning calm.
