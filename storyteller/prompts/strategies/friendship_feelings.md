Strategy for friendship and feelings stories:
- Center the story on one relationship and one small, relatable emotional problem: feeling left out, nervous, jealous, or misunderstood.
- Let the listener see the feeling through what the character does before anyone names it.
- Resolve it through an act of kindness, honesty, or understanding between the friends.
- Keep the emotional temperature gentle. No grief, loss, or serious conflict; the warmth of the friendship is the heart of the story.
- End with the friends together, safe and content, as the day closes.
