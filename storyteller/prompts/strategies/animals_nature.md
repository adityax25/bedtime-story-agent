Strategy for animals and nature stories:
- Let animals keep their real behaviors and habitats while still having feelings and voices.
- Weave in one true, delightful fact about the animal or natural world (how owls turn their heads, why leaves change color) so the child learns something without a lesson.
- Use rich sensory detail from nature: rustling leaves, cool moss, the smell of rain.
- The conflict comes from the natural world in a gentle way: a long migration, a storm passing, finding a cozy burrow.
- End with the animals settling into their nests, dens, or burrows as night falls.
