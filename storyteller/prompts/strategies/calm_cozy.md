Strategy for calm and cozy stories:
- Keep conflict minimal; the story is a gentle journey rather than a problem to solve.
- Fill it with soft sensory detail: warm blankets, soft light, quiet sounds, slow breathing.
- Use a soothing, rhythmic structure with repetition, such as visiting a series of friends who are each getting ready for sleep.
- Let the pace slow steadily from the first beat to the last.
- The wind-down is the longest part of the story, ending with everyone, including the listener, drifting off to sleep.
